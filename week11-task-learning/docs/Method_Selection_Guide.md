# Method Selection Guide

## Hypothesis Test
Use when:
- you want to compare groups or test a clear statistical claim

In this repo:
- the example compares average salary for jobs that require Python versus jobs that do not

What to explain:
- the null hypothesis
- the alternative hypothesis
- why the outcome is continuous
- why the groups are independent
- what the p-value means

## Linear Regression
Use when:
- you want to explain or predict a continuous outcome

In this repo:
- average salary is the outcome

What to explain:
- which predictors you chose
- coefficient direction
- significance
- model fit
- assumptions and diagnostics

## Classification
Use when:
- the target is a category or binary label

In this repo:
- the example predicts whether a role is a data scientist role

What to explain:
- the target variable
- train/test split
- confusion matrix
- accuracy, precision, recall, specificity, and F1

## Clustering
Use when:
- you want to find natural groupings without a target variable

In this repo:
- the example uses salary, ratings, company age, and skill indicators

What to explain:
- why features were scaled
- how you chose `k`
- what makes the clusters different
- how the clusters help interpretation
